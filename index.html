TrES-2

This chart plots relative flux—how bright the star looks compared to normal— versus time around a transit, when exoplanet TrES-2 passes in front of the star and blocks a small fraction of its light. Values near 1 are normal brightness; the dip in the middle is the transit. Hover for measurements, toggle the model line, or zoom in to explore the shape.
